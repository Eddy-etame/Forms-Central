# 02 · Product: features that make someone pay

> Lens P. 39 product ideas across the lead inbox, AI, alerts, reliability, agency features, integrations and form capabilities.
> It is one of five research lenses behind the rebuild plan ([`README.md`](README.md)). Written on 2026-09-28 by an independent research agent, with live web research and code references; every URL was accessed that day. **[HYPOTHESIS]** marks an unverified assumption, and "secondary" marks a third-party source.
> Court references (A1–A9, B1–B6, N1–N23, §5) point to `docs/tribunal/05-verdict.md`.

---

I proposed 39 ideas across the 7 required categories. Every URL was accessed on 2026-09-28, and every `file:line` was re-read in this repo. Nothing was written to the repo, and the live site was not touched.

**Pricing assumptions used in the "Revenue logic" column**
- **E1 (the studio line):** about €12 per site per month, within the €10–15 range.
- **E2 (the agency plan):** €39 per month with 10 end-clients included, then €3 for each extra end-client. The €3 figure comes from the Economist's "$3 per client" suggestion, which is itself unverified (`docs/tribunal/02-economist.md:52`), so it is **[HYPOTHESIS]**.
- **E3 (self-serve):** €9 and €19.

**Where the verdict's timing constrains these ideas.** The verdict freezes "seats, integrations, WhatsApp, … custom-domain portals" until 2026-12-27 (`docs/tribunal/05-verdict.md:366`). Four ideas fit work the court has already ordered:
- P1 and P3 feed the monthly report, which is currently sent by hand from one SQL query (`05-verdict.md:306, 328`).
- P20 automates the daily canaries the court already requires (`05-verdict.md:305, 313`).
- P22 is that monthly report, built instead of done by hand.

Most other ideas are post-gate.

**Formspree Free: I could not settle this.** Its plans page lists Slack, Discord and Telegram as "Yes" on Free (https://formspree.io/plans). Its help centre says these plugins are "Available on Personal, Professional, & Business plans" (https://help.formspree.io/hc/en-us/articles/44775790301331-Plugins). Treat chat alerts as costing somewhere between $0 and $10 a month.

## Top 5 from this lens

Ranked by how much each raises willingness to pay per day of effort.

1. **P1 + P3, the pipeline with one-tap status (3–4 d).** Every retention and report feature depends on it, and it is what an owner opens every day. It makes the €12/site line defensible. *Formspree, Basin, Forminit, Web3Forms and splitforms don't offer it: none lists lead statuses; only $60–95 tracking tools do.*
2. **P22 + P2, the branded monthly report with € won (3.5 d, after B5).** It turns the report the verdict already sends by hand into €3 per client, against AgencyAnalytics at $20 per client. *No form backend offers it: Formspree, Basin and Web3Forms send no end-client report, and AgencyAnalytics has reports but no form inbox.*
3. **P20 + P21, canaries plus drought and rejection alerts (3–4 d).** The verdict already requires daily canaries, and monitoring alone sells for $9–25 (FormWatch). Building it once serves both. *Formspree, Basin, Forminit and Web3Forms offer no monitoring; FormWatch and FormTracker monitor but have no inbox.*
4. **P15, WhatsApp alerts to the owner, with buttons (3–4 d plus Meta verification).** WhatsApp is the channel end-clients actually use (N22). At €3/site the margin is about 70–95%, and HighLevel charges $10 per sub-account for it. *Formspree, Basin, Forminit and Web3Forms have no WhatsApp; splitforms and Jotform alert the form owner but have no pipeline buttons.*
5. **P32, lead-source attribution (2 d).** Agencies that sell SEO or ads need proof, and CallRail and WhatConverts charge $60–95 for form attribution. *No form backend offers it natively (Formspree, Basin, Forminit, Web3Forms, splitforms).*

Sixth: **P9, AI triage (1.5 d).** It is the cheapest visible AI and reuses the existing classifier call, but Basin already has an AI lead agent on its Pro plan.

## Ideas I rejected and why

- **Official Zapier, Make and n8n apps.** Signed outbound webhooks already exist (`lib/webhooks.ts`), and Basin includes Zapier on all plans. App review costs time and adds no willingness to pay.
- **Native iOS and Android apps.** A PWA gets push since iOS 16.4 (P8). App-store review is heavy for one developer, and HighLevel's $497/mo white-label app is priced for agencies Inlet doesn't target.
- **A drag-and-drop form builder, or payment at form time.** Tally Free already has unlimited forms, logic, payments and uploads (https://tally.so/pricing), and splitforms has Stripe at $1. Inlet should only link payment to the pipeline (P36).
- **An AI chat widget on client sites.** Its cost has no ceiling, the verdict orders AI chat off (A4, N13), and the category is Podium-class ($399+/mo).
- **SMS as the main alert channel.** It costs $0.08 per SMS to France, the US requires 10DLC registration, and WhatsApp ($0.0034–0.03) and push (€0) are cheaper.
- **A health or clinic vertical.** It brings GDPR Art. 9, French health-data hosting rules and HIPAA liability for a solo seller.
- **A public status page with SLA credits.** It is not billable and creates liability. Publish the delivery figure inside P22 instead.
- **Enriching individual people (LinkedIn and similar lookups).** It needs a GDPR lawful basis Inlet doesn't have. Company domains only (P13).
- **Packaging the relay as an npm package.** A2 already fixes the author's own relays, and packaging raises distribution, not willingness to pay.
