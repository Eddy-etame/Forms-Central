## Seven things this lens found beyond the trial record
All checked on 2026-09-28.
1. **AI assistants are now the top discovery channel for forms.** Tally (22 Sep 2026): "43% of new users discover Tally through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity". Its MCP server plus Tally AI crossed 1M tool calls in one month (https://blog.tally.so/6-years-in-6-million-far/). **But `/llms.txt` is not what drives this.** In an Ahrefs study of 137k sites, 97% of llms.txt files got zero traffic (https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/).
2. **Vercel Marketplace lists exactly one form backend.** It is Formspree, with "1000+ installs", listed as a connectable-account integration (https://vercel.com/marketplace/formspree). Since 6 Aug 2026, Marketplace installs also install the provider's agent skills from skills.sh (https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-marketplace-agent-skills).
3. **The one loop aimed at business owners points to a dead page.** Portal invites and client welcome emails link to the 404 domain (`emails/PortalUserWelcome.tsx:109`, `emails/ClientWelcome.tsx:107`). The portal "Powered by" line only shows on Solo: Free has `endClientLimit: 0` and Pro and above are white-label (`lib/plans.ts:55,83`; `app/portal/(protected)/layout.tsx:54-57`).
4. **Attribution is broken twice.**
   - The beacon sends only `pathname` (`components/Tracker.tsx:19`).
   - The server also strips query strings (`app/api/track/route.ts:36-37`).
   - So the existing `?ref=autoreply` and `?ref=notify` tags can never be counted.
5. **European English buyers get French.**
   - Germany, the Netherlands, Spain and Italy are served French by IP (`lib/i18n.ts:14-21`). Google says "Don't use IP analysis to adapt your content", and rates URL parameters for locales "Not recommended" (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites).
   - The live canonical is `/?lang=en`, while the sitemap `<loc>` is `/`.
   - The sitemap lists `/client/signup` (`app/sitemap.ts:15`), but the live robots.txt disallows `/client`.
6. **WordPress is where French demand actually is.** WordPress is 65.2% of .fr sites with a known CMS (verdict N21). Contact Form 7 has 10M+ installs, WP Mail SMTP 4M+ ("Is your WordPress site not sending emails? You're not alone"), and CFDB7 600k+ (installed only to keep a copy of submissions). Google suggest confirms "wordpress formulaire de contact ne fonctionne pas". It returns nothing for "backend formulaire".
7. **Every shelf requires legal pages and a real domain.** That includes Vercel, the Claude directory, OpenAI, WordPress.org and Webflow. So trust assets are a prerequisite for distribution, not polish.

## Ideas I rejected and why
1. **Paid search.** Break-even cost per click is $0.23 (`02-economist.md` §3.3). Developer-tool clicks cost far more.
2. **AppSumo lifetime deal.**
   - About 10% of products are approved, and AppSumo keeps roughly 60–70% (https://appsumo.com/blog/appsumo-myths).
   - Buyers are deal hunters, not agencies, and a one-person email service would carry lifetime obligations.
3. **More agent markdown files or `/llms.txt` work.** 97% of llms.txt files get zero traffic (Ahrefs). The lever is being cited on third-party pages, not publishing more files.
4. **French SEO for "backend de formulaire".** No autocomplete demand (N21, re-run 2026-09-28: no suggestions).
5. **The auto-reply "Powered by" as the main loop.** It reaches consumers, not buyers: about 0.025 paid/month (`02-economist.md:167`).
6. **Getting listed as a Lovable connector.** No public submission path; the partner page only offers Experts, Solution and Affiliate tracks (https://lovable.dev/partners/integration).
7. **Hosting-provider partnerships (o2switch, OVH, Infomaniak) and a Shopify app.**
   - I found no partner-listing path for a solo SaaS, and hosts' customers are WordPress users, better reached by D8 **[HYPOTHESIS]**.
   - Shopify has native contact forms and no hand-coding agencies.

**Limits.** My web-search budget ran out near the end. The France Num and G2/Capterra listing mechanics were not verified. Plugin install rates and AI-directory connection rates are marked **[HYPOTHESIS]**. The domain checks are registry lookups, not trademark clearance.
